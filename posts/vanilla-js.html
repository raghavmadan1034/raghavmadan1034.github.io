Vanilla JavaScript Is Enough

June 5, 2026 · Raghav

This blog renders its post list, filters it as you type and remembers your theme choice — in well under 150 lines of plain JavaScript and zero dependencies.

Rendering a list

list.innerHTML = posts
  .map(post => `<li class="post-card">${post.title}</li>`)
  .join("");

Escape anything that comes from user input before it reaches innerHTML, or build nodes with textContent instead.

Persisting preferences

localStorage.setItem("blogsite-theme", "dark");
document.documentElement.setAttribute("data-theme", "dark");

When to reach for more

Shared client-side state across many views, routing, or a large team are all good reasons to adopt a framework. A three-page blog is not one of them.
